App Name: LinkDigest
Version: 1.0.0
Platform: Android / iOS
Category: AI Productivity / Reading
Target Market: Global (busy professionals, researchers, students)
Price Model: Free 15 links/month + .99/month Pro

Description:
Paste any article, blog post, or YouTube URL and immediately get a clean
3-point summary tailored to your profession or reading level.
LinkDigest is for people who need to stay informed but have no time.

Key Features:
- Paste URL → instant 3-point summary in under 10 seconds
- Profession filter: Engineer, Marketer, Executive, Student, General
- Reading level selector: Simple, Intermediate, Expert
- Supports: Web articles, YouTube (via transcript), Reddit, Medium, PDFs
- Save summaries to personal digest library with search
- Share summary as image card or plain text
- Daily digest: auto-summarize links from your newsletter/browser history
- Android share extension: share any link from Chrome to LinkDigest
- Offline library of saved summaries

Monetization:
- Free: 15 links/month, 2 professions
- Pro (.99/month): unlimited, all professions, PDF support, share cards, digest

Target Audience: Consultants, researchers, newsletter writers, lifelong learners
Competitors: Readwise Reader, TLDR This, Elicit, Perplexity
Unique Value: Profession-targeted summaries — not generic AI summaries
